A capability-secure systems language.
Every effect needs an unforgeable cap token.
The compiler checks source ahead of time.
Zero ambient authority.

Caps

Fourteen tokens: filesystem, network, process, GPU, FFI.
A network call takes &NetCap. No token, no effect.
Fail closed (E_CAP).
